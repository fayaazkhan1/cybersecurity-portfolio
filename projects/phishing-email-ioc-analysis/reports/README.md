## Reporting Approach

The final report intentionally distinguishes:

- Observed evidence
- Automated findings
- Threat-intelligence context
- Analyst interpretation
- Final verdict
- Evidence limitations

This prevents automated reputation data or a source-dataset label from being treated as a substitute for analyst reasoning.
